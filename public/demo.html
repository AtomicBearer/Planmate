Try the whole journey

Open the same invitation in two browsers or on two devices. Each friend has their own response; the organizer makes the final decisions.

  1. When can we go?

    Create a shared trip, invite a friend, then propose date windows in Dates & attendance. Answer from both browsers. Show common availability and choose the final date explicitly.

  2. What suits everyone?

    Save each member’s trip interests and budget in Plans that fit. Propose places from Explore places, respond Interested, Maybe or Pass, and discuss an idea in its comments.

  3. What happens next?

    Compare the Relaxed, More activities and Lower estimated cost drafts. Review the reasons and missing information, then explicitly apply a draft. Show the itinerary, travel buffers, reorder preview and PDF export.

  4. Who is coming?

    Reply I’m in or Maybe separately from place votes, then let the organizer confirm the plan. Make a material itinerary change to demonstrate the request to reconfirm.

  5. How did the outing go?

    Use Today’s plan for a trip happening today, or select a past trip day. Mark a stop visited, record an actual bill and a repayment, then add your own photo and caption in Our scrapbook.

What’s connected here?

These are configuration checks. A configured service still needs a successful live test; this page does not make billable provider requests.

Checking this server…

    Availability, votes, draft recommendations and expense arithmetic use Planmate’s own rules. An external AI service is not required. Shared updates and private uploads need the running server.

    Show the promise honestly

    The sample plan, friends and costs are labelled demonstrations. Starter places are references, not verified live inventory. Unknown venue prices, opening hours, travel and ticket availability stay unknown until a real source supplies them.

    Use supplied estimates in your own trip. Connected routes are checked estimates, and fixed event times keep their published times. The group remains in control of every plan change.
